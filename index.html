    外派人員請辭需向境外母實體與台灣單位同步提出，在台工作許可即刻失效需限期離境。Resignation terminates Class A permits immediately; expats must depart within 10-15 days.
    • 居留寬限期Grace Period: 居留事由消失後，移民署通常限令 10~15 日內離境或轉換簽證。Immigration authorities mandate departure within 10-15 days post-cancellation.
    • 競業效力Non-Compete: 全球性競業限制條款受歐洲母國法規嚴格審查約束。Restrictive covenants are governed and evaluated under home-country codes.
    法源：《入出國及移民法》§31；母國契約法規

    供應鏈挖角與稅務滯納Supply Chain Poaching & Taxes
    痛點：技術總監被競爭對手挖角，利用在台人脈私下轉移供應商；離境未繳稅使公司受罰。Pain Point: Poached directors misappropriate local subcontractor networks; untracked taxes penalize sponsors.
    • 落實 Non-solicitation 條款，嚴禁離職後私下招攬專案下包商。Enforce strict non-solicitation clauses barring recruitment of local suppliers.
    • 未完成所得稅申報擅自離境，境內實體承擔扣繳義務人補稅與罰款。Departures without tax clearance trigger secondary withholding penalties on sponsors.

    MLC 2006 海事公約規範MLC 2006 Standards
    《2006 年海事勞工公約》與船旗國法規主導：特種施工船船員直接遵循國際公約勞動條件保障。Offshore construction crew operate strictly under MLC 2006 and Flag State jurisdiction.
    • 強制休息時數Mandatory Rest: 24 小時內休息不少於 10 小時，7 天內不少於 77 小時，嚴禁造假。Min 10 hours rest per 24 hours and 77 hours per 7 days; falsification is penalized.
    • 換班通報Crew Change: 進港更替人員需依航港局與移民署流程辦理登輪與岸休許可。Crew exchanges require maritime agency filings, Seaman's Books, and shore leave permits.
    法源：ILO MLC 2006；IMO STCW 公約；港口國管制 (PSC)

    • 嚴格稽核船員仲介公司，落實「零仲介費政策 (Employer Pays)」。Audit manning brokers to enforce the Employer Pays Principle with zero illegal fees.

    海員終止合約與法定遣返MLC Repatriation Duty
    依 MLC 2006 規範，船東必須無條件承擔海員遣返至母國或登輪港之全額費用。Under MLC 2006, shipowners are strictly liable for crew repatriation to home countries.
    • 無償遣返權益Free Repatriation: 包含機票、途中膳宿與醫療費，嚴禁自薪資或押金扣除。Includes airfare, meals, lodging, and medical care; zero payroll deductions allowed.
    • 提前解約補償Severance Pay: 非因重大過失終止時，SEA 合約通常約定 1~2 個月工資補償。Standard SEAs mandate 1-2 months base wages unless discharged for gross fault.
    法源：ILO MLC 2006 Reg 2.5；ITF 集體勞動協議

    欠薪滯留與船舶扣押痛點Detention & Safe Manning
    痛點：專案提前結束外籍船員怠工或向 PSC 申訴，引發施工船舶遭正式扣押。Pain Point: Crew disputes lead to Port State Control interventions and formal ship detentions.
    • PSC 檢查官確認違反 MLC 勞動條件或欠薪，將依法留置船舶禁止出港。PSC officers detain ships immediately upon verifying wage arrears or MLC breaches.
    • 替換船員必須具備合格 STCW 認證，避免安全配員不足（Safe Manning）受罰。Replacement crew must hold valid STCW certificates to prevent Minimum Safe Manning violations.

    老闆想省成本 = 專案提前結束將船員丟在港口，要求仲介自行買機票。Trap: Stranding mariners at local ports to force brokers to fund air tickets.

    海員合約期滿與離船Seafarer Sign-Off
    海員合約（SEA）屆滿時依法下船，即使自願離職，船東仍負擔將船員送回母國之全部旅費。Seafarers disembark upon SEA expiration; owners must fund travel home regardless.
    • 約滿遣返End-of-Tour: 常態約滿下船，雇主承擔返航機票與登陸港口必要交通。Shipowners cover flight bookings back to domicile upon normal contract completion.
    • 人道提前解約Compassionate Leave: 因直系親屬重大事故申請離船，船東應於下一個靠港口協助更替。Emergency compassionate exits must be facilitated at the next convenient port.
    法源：MLC 2006 Standard A2.5；標準 SEA 合約

    缺員停航與脫逃滯留風險Safe Manning & Desertion
    痛點：關鍵輪機手或起重手在施工期請辭，導致未達最低配員標準無法出海。Pain Point: Key winch drivers resigning mid-campaign breaches Minimum Safe Manning Certificates.
    • 仲介公司需維持儲備人才庫，確保 48~72 小時內調派合格人員抵台登輪。Maintain standby pools to mobilize STCW-certified reliefs within 48-72 hours.
    • 船員岸休逾期未歸（Desertion）需第一時間通報移民署防範非法滯留。Report unreturned crew immediately to immigration authorities to mitigate fines.
